ABOUT ME

A bit about me

I'm Zaid, an animator at Devan Studio. I make Minecraft animation in Blender, from short comedy sketches for YouTube Shorts to longer cinematic series with recurring characters, dialogue and action.

I like owning a shot from start to finish. I plan the layout and cameras, block the key poses, polish the motion and faces, light the scene and add the effects. Then I edit it and add sound until it's ready to upload.

My favourite moments to animate are the big ones: sword fights, chases and powerful VFX like glowing auras and dark energy bursts. I care just as much about the small acting moments that make a joke land.
